About the company

What we do

DIZS Software Services Pvt. Ltd. presents software services for educational institutions and businesses. The services listed on this website include school fee management, attendance and report cards, transport management, paper design, website building, and app building.

OnlineDataTool.com is the company’s online tools area. It provides browser-based utilities for working with SQL, JSON, CSV and Excel data, images, text, and common developer formats. Tools are designed to make routine tasks such as formatting, comparison, conversion, and cleanup easier to perform.

Our approach

We focus on practical software that supports everyday work. Online utilities are grouped by task so visitors can find a suitable tool, use it in a modern browser, and review the result before putting it into a project or workflow.
