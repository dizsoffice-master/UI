Lesson 10: Model cash flows and inspect results

For loan functions, keep rate and period units consistent: monthly payments require an annual rate divided by 12 and years multiplied by 12. Excel often returns cash paid as a negative value and cash received as positive; use a consistent sign convention.

For a 250,000 loan at 6% annual interest for 20 years, monthly payment is =-PMT(6%/12,20*12,250000), approximately 1,791.08.

Final project: monthly sales report

  1. Import order rows with date, region, product ID, quantity, and amount.
  2. Clean text with TRIM and validate required fields with IF and ISBLANK.
  3. Return product names with XLOOKUP and calculate line totals with ROUND.
  4. Summarize revenue by month and region using SUMIFS; count orders with COUNTIFS.
  5. Show unique regions with UNIQUE and filtered high-value orders with FILTER.
  6. Compare this month with the prior month using date boundaries and a clear percentage-change formula.
  7. Audit the result with formulas, named ranges, validation checks, and a separate assumptions sheet.

Use Trace Precedents, Evaluate Formula, and error checks before sharing. Protect inputs and document assumptions. Test the workbook with empty, duplicate, boundary, and error cases.
