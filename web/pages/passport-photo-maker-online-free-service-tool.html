Passport Photo Maker for Photo Studios and Print Shops

Prepare passport-size photos for walk-in customers. Select India, USA, UK, or Australia reference dimensions, reposition the portrait crop, choose how many copies to place on A4, then download PNG, JPG, SVG or print a sheet.

Passport Photo Printing Workflow for Photo Shops

This browser-based passport photo maker helps a photo studio, local print shop, cyber cafe, or ID photo counter prepare a customer proof and multiple-copy A4 sheet. Crop positioning, copy count, sheet layout, and downloads run locally in the browser.

Country Photo Size Presets

The preset list provides common reference dimensions: India, UK, and Australia at 35 × 45 mm, and USA at 51 × 51 mm. Passport, visa, and identity-photo rules may differ by application, authority, background, head size, and recency. Verify the current official specification before printing or submitting a final photo.

A4 Multiple-Copy Sheet

Choose the number of prints and the maker arranges copies horizontally across an A4 portrait sheet, then continues on the next row. Export the individual crop or full sheet as PNG, JPG, or SVG, or open the browser print dialog to select a printer and verify paper size and scaling.
