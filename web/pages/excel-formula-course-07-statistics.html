Lesson 7: Select a statistic that answers the question

The mean is sensitive to extreme values; the median is the middle observation. Sample functions estimate a population from observed data, while population functions treat the listed values as the entire population.

For 10, 12, 13, 15, 100, the average is 30 but the median is 13. Report both when outliers matter.

Use statistics responsibly

Correlation describes association, not causation. Check that paired ranges have the same length and aligned observations. State units, sample size, and the population/sample assumption beside a result.
